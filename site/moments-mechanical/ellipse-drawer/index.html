Ellipse Drawer

Computers in my high school had embedded GeoGebra. I liked coding linkage simulation with it. Once, I tracked the trajectory of the coupler in a double-slider mechanism. It gave me a series of ellipses. To verify the simulation, I built this prototype, and it did draw a ellipse.

Next day, I learnt in Shanghai Library: this was actually a Trammel of Archimedes
